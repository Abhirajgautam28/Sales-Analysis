# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our project follows the Contributor Covenant code of conduct. By participating,
you agree to abide by its terms. Please be welcoming and respectful to all
project contributors.

## Key points

- Be kind and professional.
- Provide constructive feedback and be receptive to feedback on your work.
- Avoid exclusionary or harassing behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the
project maintainers at `abhirajgautam28@users.noreply.github.com`.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant, see [https://www.contributor-covenant.org](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
